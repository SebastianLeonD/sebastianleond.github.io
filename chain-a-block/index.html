Chain-a-Block

A chain-matching puzzle game for iPhone.

Need help? Email [[EMAIL]] with your iPhone model and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone only. There are no accounts, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone without a device backup starts you over.

How do I start over?
Settings (the gear) › Erase everything.

Why does the app ask to track me?
The game is free and paid for by ads. Allowing tracking only makes the ads more relevant; the game works the same either way. Change it any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

A watch-an-ad button disappeared.
It only shows when an ad is ready to play. Check your connection and try again in a minute.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Settings (the gear) in the game.
