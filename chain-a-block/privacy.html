Privacy Policy

Chain-a-Block · effective November 8, 2026

The short version. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics. Your progress never leaves your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

Chain-a-Block is made by Sebastian Leon ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: [[EMAIL]].

What the game stores, and where

Your scores, coins, boosters, event progress and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. There is no sign-in and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob, which runs inside the app. To show ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure results, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below), and other device or app-scoped identifiers;
  • approximate location worked out from your IP address;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • crash and performance data from the ad software.

Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iPhone asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you still get ads, just less personalised ones. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. You are shown Google's consent form before any ad loads. You can change your answer later with Ad privacy choices on the game's Settings screen.
  • Delete your data. Settings › Erase everything in the game wipes all saved progress. Deleting the app removes everything too. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else for us to delete.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through Google may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under laws such as California's. You can opt out by turning tracking off as described above. You may also contact us to exercise any right you have under your state's law, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

Children

Chain-a-Block is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data was collected through the app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
