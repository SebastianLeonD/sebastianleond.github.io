Privacy Policy

Chain-a-Block · effective November 15, 2026

The short version. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics, and your progress never leaves your phone. The game is paid for by ads. The ad software inside the app, from Google AdMob and Unity Ads, collects some device data to show and measure those ads. You can limit that with the choices described below.

Who we are

Chain-a-Block is made by Sebastian Leon, an individual developer based in Florida, United States ("we", "us"). We decide how the app works and which ad partners are in it, so for data-protection purposes we are the party responsible for the app, and Sebastian Leon is the person responsible for protecting personal information. Contact: contact.chainablock@gmail.com.

What we collect ourselves

  • From playing the game: nothing. Your scores, coins, boosters, castle and event progress and settings are saved only on your device. So are the counts the game uses to space out ads (when you first opened the game, how many runs you have played and how many ads you were shown today) and the way it eases difficulty when your timer runs low. None of it is sent to us. If you back up your iPhone, Apple may include this saved data in your backup under your own Apple account.
  • If you email us: your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and delete it within two years of our last reply.
  • From Apple and our ad partners: totals that do not identify anyone, such as how many people downloaded the game, crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers, and how many ads were shown and what they earned.

There is no sign-in. We never ask for your name, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Ads: what our partners collect

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. For each ad Google runs an auction among advertisers, and Unity Ads takes part in it. Both companies' ad software runs inside the app, and they work with other advertising companies (advertisers, and the ad exchanges and measurement companies that serve them). To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, prevent fraud and measure results, this software may collect:

  • identifiers: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking; an identifier that is specific to this app on your device; and identifiers the ad partners assign themselves;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location (city or region) worked out from it;
  • device details: model, operating system, language, screen size, connection type;
  • ad activity: which ads you were shown, watched or tapped, and whether you installed or bought something after an ad;
  • diagnostics: crash and performance data from the ad software.

This data may also be used to show you personalised ads, based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Where the law requires your permission for that, you are asked first; elsewhere it happens unless you opt out as described below. We do not receive any of it in a form that identifies you. We use only ad partners whose agreements with us and published policies require them to protect this data at least as well as this policy describes. The partners handle it under their own policies, where you can also ask them for a copy of your data or its deletion:

  • Google: Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services;
  • Unity: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking (everyone). iPhone asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared; you still see ads, but they are not personalised using it. Change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The game works the same either way and gives no reward for allowing it.
  • Ad privacy choices (where the law gives you one). In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland you are shown a consent form before any ad is requested, and no ad is requested if you have not answered it. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. In both cases a button called Ad privacy choices is added to the game's Settings screen, where you can change your answer at any time.
  • Erase your game data. Settings › Erase everything in the game wipes your progress (scores, coins, boosters, castle and events). Your sound and vibration settings and the ad-spacing counts stay until you delete the app, which removes everything the game stored. We hold no copy.

US state privacy rights (California and others)

In the last 12 months the ad software in the app may have collected these categories of personal information from players, for the advertising purposes described above: identifiers, internet or other electronic network activity (ad activity), commercial information (whether an ad led to an install or purchase), approximate geolocation, and inferences drawn from them by the ad partners. It comes from your device. We collect none of the sensitive categories.

We do not sell personal information for money. Letting ad partners use it for personalised ads can count as "selling", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under laws such as California's. You have the right to opt out: use Ad privacy choices on the game's Settings screen. A choice made there is honoured by Google and passed on to Unity Ads as an opt-out. An app cannot receive browser signals such as Global Privacy Control, so please use that button. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

Depending on your state you may also have the right to know what was collected, to get a copy, to correct it and to have it deleted, and the right not to be treated differently for using these rights. Because we hold no data that identifies you, the quickest route is the partners' own tools linked above; you can also email us and we will help. You may use an authorised agent, and you may appeal a decision by replying to our email.

UK, EEA and Switzerland

The ad partners store and read information on your device, and use personal data for ads, only on the basis of the consent you give in the consent form. You can withdraw it at any time with Ad privacy choices on the Settings screen. We answer emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in supporting players.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to your data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk). Email us to use these rights.

We and the ad partners are based in the United States and elsewhere, so data is processed outside your country. Google and Unity describe in their own policies the safeguards they rely on for those transfers, such as standard contractual clauses and the Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept, and how it is protected

Game data stays on your device until you erase it or delete the app. Emails are deleted within two years of our last reply. The ad partners keep what they collect for the periods set out in their own policies. We run no servers, so there is no database of players for anyone to break into; the ad partners send data over encrypted connections.

Children

Chain-a-Block is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and the App Store rates it 13+. Ads are limited to content rated for teens: Google's Teen rating, and no ads rated 17+ from Unity Ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's data was collected through the app, email us and we will help get it removed.
